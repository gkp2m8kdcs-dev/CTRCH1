Privacy Policy

CTRCH · Last updated: October 1, 2026

CTRCH is built around a single promise: your financial data stays on your device. We do not run a backend, we do not have an account system, and we do not sell, rent or trade anything you put into the app.

1. Information we collect

CTRCH does not collect personal information automatically. The only data the app stores is the data you choose to enter while using it — for example, account names, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring bills and receipt photos. All of this is saved locally on your iPhone.

2. How we use that information

Locally stored data is used only to power the features you see inside CTRCH: rendering the dashboard, charts, budgets, goals and recurring bills. None of that data is sent to us, to a third-party processor, or to any analytics platform.

3. Camera and photo library

CTRCH will only request camera or photo-library access at the moment you tap the button to add a receipt attachment to a transaction. iOS will show a permission prompt the first time. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy → Photos or Settings → Privacy → Camera on your iPhone. Photos attached to a transaction are stored inside the app sandbox on your device.

4. Notifications

CTRCH does not send you push notifications and does not request notification permission. Recurring-bill reminders are computed inside the app and shown when you open it.

5. Backups you export

CTRCH lets you manually export a JSON backup to the Files app. That file lives wherever you choose to put it — on your device, in iCloud Drive, or in a third-party storage provider. We have no access to that file once it leaves the app.

6. Third-party services

CTRCH does not embed third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting or marketing SDKs. The app does not contact any external server during normal use.

7. Children

CTRCH is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Your rights and how to delete your data

Because CTRCH does not store data on any server, deleting your data is entirely in your control:

  • Delete a record inside the app at any time from its detail view.
  • Restore from an older backup to roll the whole app back to a previous state.
  • Delete the CTRCH app from your iPhone to erase all locally stored data.
  • Delete exported backup files from iCloud Drive or the Files app whenever you choose.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, when the change is material, surface a notice inside the app.
