Getting started

1. Create your first account

Open the Accounts tab and tap the plus button. Choose an account type — Checking, Savings, Credit Card, Investment or Loan — give it a name, currency, and optional opening balance. Your account will appear on the dashboard immediately.

2. Log a transaction

From the Transactions tab, tap the plus button. Pick the type (Income, Expense or Transfer), the account, the amount and a category. You can attach a photo of a receipt straight from the camera or your photo library — the image stays on your device.

3. Plan ahead

The Plans tab is where you set monthly budgets per category, define savings or debt-payoff goals with contribution tracking, and list recurring bills. CTRCH will surface bills that are overdue or due soon so nothing slips through.

4. Back up your data

Everything in CTRCH lives in a local JSON file. Open Settings → Backup & Restore to save a snapshot to the Files app, and use Restore to load it back on the same or another device. We recommend exporting a backup once a month.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All transactions, accounts, budgets and goals live in a file inside the app sandbox on your iPhone. CTRCH does not run a server, does not have an account system and does not connect to your bank.

Do you collect any analytics or telemetry?

No. CTRCH does not include third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporters or advertising frameworks. When you contact support and send us a message, we only see the information you put in the email.

Can I use CTRCH with multiple currencies?

Yes. Open Settings → Currencies to pick a base, then change the reference rate for any of the 20+ supported currencies. All dashboard totals will be converted to your base currency.

I lost my phone. Can you restore my backup?

We cannot — and that is by design. Backups are regular JSON files in your Files app or iCloud Drive, so as long as you have exported one in the past, you can import the backup into a fresh install of CTRCH on your new device.

Why is the receipt camera asking for access?

iOS requires explicit permission to use the camera or photo library. CTRCH only asks when you tap the camera or photo button while attaching a receipt. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy.

The dashboard figures look off by a few cents.

That is usually caused by rounding when converting to the base currency. Open Settings → Currencies, refresh the reference rates, or change the base currency to match the currency most of your accounts are held in.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the CTRCH app from your iPhone and all locally stored data is removed with it. If you previously exported a backup, you can delete the file from iCloud Drive or the Files app at any time.
